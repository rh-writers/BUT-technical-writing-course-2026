= How to make a borscht

== First, be sure that you have all ingredients prepared
* beef 500g
* beet roots 1pc 
* cabbage 200g 
* potatoes 2pc 
* carrots 1 pc
* onions 1 pc
* tomato paste 
* butter 2 spoons 
* bay leaf 1-2pc 
* pepper 
* salt 
* water 1,5 liters 
* dill twigs 2-3
* sour cream 2 spoons 

= Instructions

== 1. Prepare the broth.
* Place the meat in a pot, cover it with water, and bring to a boil.
* Skim off any foam, reduce the heat, and simmer for 1–1.5 hours (about 40 minutes for chicken).
* Remove the cooked meat and cut it into bite-sized pieces.

== 2. Prepare the vegetables.
* Dice the potatoes.
* Finely shred the cabbage.
* Grate the beetroot and carrot using a coarse grater.
* Finely chop the onion.

== 3. Make the vegetable sauté.
* Sauté the onion in oil for 3–4 minutes.
* Add the carrot and cook for another 3 minutes.
* Add the beetroot and tomato paste.
* Pour in a few spoonfuls of broth and simmer for 10–15 minutes.

== 4. Assemble the borscht.
* Add the potatoes to the boiling broth and cook for 10 minutes.
* Add the cabbage and cook for another 10 minutes.
* Stir in the vegetable sauté.
* Return the meat to the pot and add the bay leaf, salt, and pepper.

== 5. Finish the borscht.
* Simmer for another 5–10 minutes.
* Add the minced garlic.
* Turn off the heat and let the borscht rest for 15–20 minutes.

*Serve with sour cream, fresh herbs, and dark bread if desired. Borshtch is often even more flavourful the next day.*
