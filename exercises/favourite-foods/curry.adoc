= Chicken curry with rice

== ingredients

=== For the Curry
* 700g chicken thighs or breast (cut into bite-sized pieces)
* 1 large yellow onion, chopped
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tbsp cooking oil
* 2 tbsp curry powder (your favorite store-bought blend)
* 400ml crushed tomatoes
* 1/2 cup water (or chicken broth)
* 1/2 tsp salt (adjust to taste)

=== For the Rice
* 1 cup white rice
* 2 cups water
* 1 pinch of salt

== Method
Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Cook the chopped onion for 5 minutes until soft. Stir in the garlic and cook for 1 more minute until you can smell it.

Add the chicken pieces and curry powder to the pan. Stir well to coat the chicken completely in the spice. Cook for about 5 minutes until the outside of the chicken turns white/opaque.

While the curry is simmering, bring 2 cups of water, 1 cup of rice, and a pinch of salt to a boil in a separate pot. Cover with a lid, turn the heat to low, and leave it alone for 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and let it steam for 5 minutes before serving.
