= Pasta Alfredo

== Ingredients:
* 450g fettucine pasta
* 1 stick butter
* 1 cup heavy cream
* 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
* 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
* salt and black pepper
* fresh parsley

== Instructions:

. Boil the pasta: Cook the fettuccine in a large pot of salted boiling water unti it is al dente (tender but stull firm)
. Save pasta water: Before draining the pasta, scoop out and save 1/2 cup of the hot pasta water. Then, drain the pasta.
. Melt the butter: In a large skillet, mekt the butter over medium-low heat.
. Add the cream: Pour in the heavy cream and garlic powder. Let is simmet gently for 3 minutes,
. Add the cheese: Turn the heat to low. Stir in the Parmesan cheese, salt, and pepper until the cheese melts and the souce is smoouth.
. Combine: Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss well to coat the noodles.
. Thicken the sauce: Add a splash of the reserved pasta water to the skillet if the sauce is too thick.
. Serve: Garnish with chopped parsley and serve immediately
