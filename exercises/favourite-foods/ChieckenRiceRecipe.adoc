== Ingridients 
----
  1 Chicken breast, 100grams of jasmine rice, salt, paper, olive oil.
----

  
. Season the chicken breast with salt, paper and a tea spoon of olive oil.
. Put it in oven for 25 min, 220 degrees.
. Wash the rice 3-4 times then pour 200 ml of clean water and add a pintch of salt.
. Let it simmer in a ricecooker for 12 min.
