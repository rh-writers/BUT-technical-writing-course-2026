= Chicken and rice for 5 days

- Easy, straight forward meal preperation of a super plain food, but it does the work

== Ingredients:
- 500 g of rice = 5 cups (whatever type you prefer)
- 750 g of chicken breasts
- 500 g of broccoli
- 5 cups of water
- oil
- salt
- black pepper

== Tools:
- pan
- pot
- lunch boxes

== Procedure:
. Prepare your pot and put your rice in it
. Pour water into the pot and season the water with salt (3 table spoons)
. Stir the water with rice and salt and put it on high heat stove for 15 minutes
. Cut your meat into small cube looking like pieces 
. Heat up your pan on stove and pour about 4 table spoons of oil on it
. Season your meat with salt and pepper
. Put the meat on the pan and cook it until done
. After 5 minutes add your broccoli to the pan and cook it until the broccoli is soft
. After the meat and broccoli is done and the rice is coocked, turn off the heat
. Make portions for 5 lunch boxes (100g rice, 100g broccoli, 150g chicken breasts)
. Let it cool for a bit and then put it into the fridge 
--
- Great job! You just made yourself 5 days worth of food for your lunches.
