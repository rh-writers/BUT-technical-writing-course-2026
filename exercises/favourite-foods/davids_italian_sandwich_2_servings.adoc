= Recipe for 2 servings of Davids' favourite italian sandwich

=== Needed ingredients:
--------
* 1 Focaccia (or any other italian bread with herbs)
* 2 tsp of Olive oil
* 4 tsp of Truffle mayo
* 2 slices of Gouda cheese
* 4 slices of Prosciutto Crudo or Mortadella
* Handful of Washed arugula
* 1 Medium sized tomato
* 1/3 tsp of Salt
* 1/3 tsp of Pepper
* 2 1/2 tsp of balsamic vinegar
* 1 Mozzarella cheese
--------

=== Needed tools:
--------
* Teaspoon
* Large skillet
* 2 Medium sized plates
* Bread knife
* Medium sized straight-blade knife
* Cutting board
--------

=== Procedure:
--------
1.   Heat the Skillet on medium setting on  and pour the Olive oil on it
2.   Place the Foccacia on the Cutting Board and slice it horizontally in half
     with the bread knife
3.   Place the sliced Foccacia on the Skillet (sliced side down) for 5 minutes. 
     (or until the Foccacia is golden brown)
4.   Take the Truffle mayo and spread 2 tsp of it on the cooked side of each
     Foccacia half with a Spoon
5.   Take the Gouda cheese and evenly spread it on the flatter half of the Foccacia
6.   Take the Arugula and evenly spread it on the half with Gouda cheese
7.   Place the Tomato on Cutting Board and slice it as thinly as possible 
     with the straight-edge knife
8.   Place the Tomato slices on the half with Gouda cheese
9.   Open the Balsamic vinegar and squeeze it upside down above the half 
     with Gouda cheese
10.  Sprinkle Salt and Pepper on the half with Gouda cheese
11.  Take the slices of Prosciutto Crudo or Mortadella and evenly spread it 
     on the half with Gouda cheese
12.  Take the Mozzarella, put it on the cutting board and slice it as thin as possible
     with the straight-edge knife
13.  Spread the Mozzarella slices evenly on the half with Gouda cheese
14.  Take the other half of the Foccacia and put it cooked side down on the rest of the sandwich
     so it copies the original shape of Foccacia
15.  Take the sandwich and put it on the Cutting Board.
16.  Cut the sandwich in halves vertically with the Bread Knife
     (make the cut from the middle, not the end of the sandwich)
17.  Serve each half of sandwich on separate plates and enjoy the sandwich
--------
