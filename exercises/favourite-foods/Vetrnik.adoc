== My Favourite Větrník
You really better go buy one than cook it yourself

=== Ingredients:

*Caramel cream:*

* 200 g butter or Hera margarine
* 750 ml milk
* 150 g powdered sugar (to taste)
* 2 packets Dr. Oetker instant caramel pudding mix


*Dough:*

* 0.50 tsp salt
* 750 ml water
* 300 g all-purpose flour
* 8 eggs
* 250 g Omega or butter

*Cream layer:*

* 2 packets vanilla sugar
* 2 packets whipped cream stabilizer - if using cream with less than 33% fat
* 500 ml whipping cream

*Caramel glaze:*

* 200 ml whipping cream
* 200 g granulated or caster sugar

=== Procedure:
*Pastry base:*

. Bring water, fat, and salt to a rolling boil in a saucepan.
. Add flour all at once. Stir vigorously over heat until the dough forms a smooth ball that pulls away from the sides of the   pan.
. Transfer to a mixer. Cool slightly, then incorporate eggs one at a time until the batter is smooth and fully emulsified.
. Divide the dough in half. Spread evenly into two baking sheets (use one deep pan for final assembly).
. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes until golden brown and firm. Cool completely.

Caramel buttercream:

. Cook the caramel pudding and milk until thickened. Place plastic wrap directly on the surface of the pudding to prevent a    skin, then cool completely.
. Cream the butter and sugar in a mixer until light and fluffy.
. Gradually mix the cooled pudding into the aerated butter until smooth and homogenous.
. Spread evenly over the bottom choux layer in the deep pan.

Cream:

. Whip the heavy cream, sugar, and stabilizer to stiff peaks.
. Spread in an even layer directly over the caramel buttercream.
. Top with the second baked choux layer, pressing down gently to level.

Caramel Glaze & Finishing

. Melt and caramelize the sugar in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring occasionally (watch closely to prevent burning).
. Carefully deglaze by pouring in warmed heavy cream (caution: the mixture will bubble rapidly).
. Whisk continuously over low heat until smooth and any hardened sugar lumps dissolve. Adjust consistency by reducing over     heat or adding a splash of cream if necessary.
. Pour the warm glaze evenly over the top pastry layer.
. Chill the assembled pastry until fully set, then portion with a sharp or serrated knife before serving.
