= A risotto recipe

== Ingredients needed:

* 250g rice
* 200g chicken
* peas
* seasoning
* salt & pepper
* oil
* cheese
----
== Tools:
* boiling pot or a rice cooker
* frying pan
* spatula
* knife
* cutting board
----
== Procedure:
1. Start cooking the rice in salted water in a pot (ideally use a rice cooker) and cook for around 15 min.
2. In the meantime start preparing your chicken by seasoning it and cutting it to small pieces.
3. Pour a little bit of oil on a frying pan and let it heat up.
4. Add your prepared chicken with peas on the pan and make sure you fry it properly. 
5. Finally add your boiled rice on the pan and stir thoroughly
6. Add a desired amount of salt, pepper and serve with shredded cheese on top
7. Bon appetit!
