= Plain Spaghetti

A simple guide for making the simplest of foods.

----
Notice: No numbers are listed in this guide as they are highly subjective to the specific preparation circumstances.
----

== Prerequisites

* Pot
* Strainer 
* Bowl or plate
* Stove
* Water
* Salt
* Spaghetti

= Procedure

. Fill the pot half-way full with water
. Add salt to the pot
. Place the pot on the stove
. Turn the stove on
. Wait until the water in the pot is boiling
. Add the spaghetti
. Make sure that the noodles are entirely submerged in water
. Leave the spaghetti boil for the amount of time stated on the packadging
. Turn off the stove
. Wait until the water stops boiling
. Move the pot and the strainer next to the sink
. Position the strainer over the sink
. Pour the pot over the strainer
. Optional: Run the spaghetti through cold water 
. Place the spagehtti on the plate or in the bowl 
. Serve
