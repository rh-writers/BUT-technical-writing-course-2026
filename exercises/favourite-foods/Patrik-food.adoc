Chicken breast with rice +


Prepare: +
Breast of chicken +
Oil (sunflower, olive whichever) +
Salt +
Grill spices +
Rice (Whichever you have home) +
Rice cooker +
Pan +
Plate +
Cuttlery +

Step by step cooking guide +

Rice +
1. Pour rice into rice cookers bowl +
2. Add as much water, that it is slightly above rice +
3. Plug rice cooker in power outlet +
4. Turn on the rice cooker +
5. Let it cook for so long till the rice cooker signals that its done cooking +
6. Take out the cookers bowl with rice +
7. Put cooked Rice on plate +

Chicken Breast +
1. Prepare a pan and put it on a stove +
2. On the pan pour a little bit of oil and turn the stove on +
3. In the meantime, place the chicken breast on cutting board +
4. Tenderize the chicken breast with a mallet +
5. Put salt and grill spices on the chicken on both sides +
6. Place chicken breast on pan that is on stove with hot oil +
7. Let chicken breast cook for 5 minutes on one side +
8. Flip the chicken breast and let it cook for another 5 minutes +
9. After its cooked, turn off the stove +
10. Put cooked chicken breast on plate with rice +
11. Enjoy +
