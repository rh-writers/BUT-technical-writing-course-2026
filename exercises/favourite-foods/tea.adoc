Two tea recipes for cold weather:

### Green Dragon in the Red Fog
---
#### Ingridients:
* good green tea with jasmine or mint - 2 tbsp per 250 ml of tea
* plant milk (pref. oat milk) - 20-40 ml per 250 ml
* grenadine syrup - to taste
* two vessels for preparation (ex. a teapot and a cup)

---
1. Brew your tea in a teapot. Note that the water temperature should not exceed 85C or else the leaves will loose their taste and gain bitterness.
2. Once the tea brewed long enough, extract the tea leaves.
3. Add your milk.
4. Add some grenadine to the cup.
5. Pour you tea concoction to a cup, thoroughly stirring.

---

### Plague buster

---
Actually, it is not directly effective against the plague. But it helps your plague-resistant immunity, so it works!

#### Ingridients:

* Rosehip with 5-angled tip (where the flower once was)
* Citrus
* Mint
* An apple, the red one
* Pepper
* Cinnamon or cloves
* Honey

 [If you have a --wet-- cough, add a little licorice to the list]

---
1. Slowly brew an apple at 80 C for 5 mins.
2. Add your citrus of choice and brew some more.
3. Lower temperature to 50-60C (you will be able to touch the water without much pain, yet it will feel hot).
4. Add mint, pepper, cinnamon or cloves and leave it for 10-20 minutes.
5. Let it chill a little (to 30-40C).
6. Add honey and drink a lot!
