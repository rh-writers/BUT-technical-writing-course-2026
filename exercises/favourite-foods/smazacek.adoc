= Preparation of smažený sýr

==Ingredients

=== For the Cheese
* *Cheese:* 4 thick slices (about 1.5 cm or 1/2 inch thick) of *Edam* (Eidam) or *Gouda*. (You can also use a wheel of Camembert/Hermelín).
* *Flour:* 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
* *Eggs:* 2 large eggs
* *Milk:* 1 tablespoon
* *Breadcrumbs:* 1 cup fine, plain breadcrumbs
* *Salt:* A small pinch (for the eggs)

== Instructions
Set up three shallow bowls or deep plates:

=== Preparation of bowls
--
* *Bowl 1:* The all-purpose flour.
* *Bowl 2:* The eggs, whisked together with the milk and a tiny pinch of salt.
* *Bowl 3:* The breadcrumbs.
--

=== Fry to Perfection
--
* Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. The oil should be hot but not smoking (around 170°C). To test it, drop a pinch of breadcrumbs into the oil; it should sizzle immediately.
* Carefully lower the cheese slices into the oil. 
* Fry for about *1.5 to 2 minutes per side*, until a deep, crispy golden brown. Do not crowd the pan; fry in batches if necessary.
--

=== Drain and Serve
--
* Remove the cheese with a spatula and briefly place it on a plate lined with paper towels to absorb any excess oil.
* *Serve immediately!* Smažený sýr is best enjoyed piping hot while the cheese is still stringy and gooey. 
--
