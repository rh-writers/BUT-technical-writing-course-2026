=Toast
-------
* we need:2 pieces of bread, sliced ham, sliced cheese, ketchup, contact grill

1. take one piece of bread
2. put a slice of cheese on the piece of bread
3. put a small amount of ketchup on the cheese on the bread
4. put a slice of ham on the cheese on the bread
5. put the second piece of bread on the ham.
6. put the whole thing inside a small contact grill
7. wait 3 to 4 minutes
8. take the toast out of the contact grill
9. enjoy
