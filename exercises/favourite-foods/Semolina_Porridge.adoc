= Simple Semolina Porridge Recipe

*Abbreviation:* tbsp (tablespoon)
You can use coarse flour instead of semolina.

== Ingredients for 2 servings

* 250 mL milk
* 4 tbsp semolina
* 2 tbsp sugar
* 1 tbsp butter
* (Optional) cocoa
* (Optional) cinnamon

== Procedure

1. Pour milk into a pot.
2. Place the pot on the stove and set the burner to high heat.
3. Wait for the milk to simmer.
4. When the milk begins to simmer, add the sugar.
5. Stir the milk continuously.
6. While stirring, slowly add the semolina. Add it gradually to prevent lumps from forming.
7. Continue stirring the mixture.
8. When the mixture thickens, remove the pot from the stove. Continue stirring for 2 minutes.
9. Pour the porridge onto a plate.
10. Place the butter on top of the porridge and allow it to melt.
11. (Optional) sprinkle the porridge with cocoa or cinnamon.
12. Serve immediately.
