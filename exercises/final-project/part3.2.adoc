=== 3.2. interacting with other writers

When you have job as a technical writer you will have to comunicate with your coworkers.

* while speaking with coworkers be polite
* before you ask try to educate yourself with information you have access to
* ask questions when you do not understand the subject you are writing about
* while asking be direct 
* when given some feedback do not make it personal
* while giving feedback to coworkers be direct and objective while not insulting
