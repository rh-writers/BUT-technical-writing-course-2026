= Employment as a Technical Writer

== Job Market and Opportunities
Technical writing is a rapidly growing field. Companies look for writers who can bridge the gap between complex engineering concepts and end-users.

=== Common Industries
* Software Development (SaaS)
* Hardware and Electronics
* Aerospace and Defense

== Career Progression
[cols="1,2", options="header"]
|===
| Role | Responsibility
| Junior Technical Writer | Creating basic user guides, updating existing documentation.
| Senior Technical Writer | Setting documentation strategy, mentoring juniors, toolchain management.
|===
