= Guide to Technical Writing


== What is technical writing?  
----
Your aim is to understand how product works, and help user to achieve their goal. 
Technical writing is used in manuals, guides, solutions, troubleshooting. 
 
Understand the subject → provide simple and imformative explanation to save work and stress for the readers.


----

== Technical docs style ≠ general writing style 

----
* Focus on readability, findability, accessibility, and ultimately the user 
 experience 
 * Some best practices: 
 * Use simple, translation-friendly language. 
 * Use visual structure and formatting to make the text easy to read. 
 * Focus on helping the reader accomplish a specific goal.
----

## How to give feedback

1. Prepare information about the topic and stick to the facts.
2. Mind your approach (tone and non-verbal cues).
3. Remain empathetic and listen to the person’s responce.
3. Focus on improvement of the piece of work you are giving feedback to.

Here are some examples:

### Positive feedback

Point to the specific part you find good.

Examples:

‘I like the structure of this module.,

‘Thank you for adding this link.,

### Asking for more information: 
1. Introduce yourself.
2. Remind of the object of the discussion (particular piece of work).
3. Ask a question.
4. Thank the person.
### Negative feedback
Stick to the facts while giving negative feedback.

1. State the fact.
2. Explain why this situation is unwanted.
3. Ask for correction or offer the solutions.

Example:
‘ This document has complicated structure, it received many complaints from the users. Can you please restructuralize it? I have a few example articles that can help you with this.’


## How to receive feedback

* Be proactive and ask for the feedback
* Remain civil
* Focus on improvements
* Express gratitude for the reviews
* Stay objective.

## Peer review
* Work with style guides
* Add references to your comments if needed
* Differentiate between required and optional changes.
* Provide positive feedback as well as negative.

Main points of focus:
* grammar
* style
* minimalism 

## Productivity
To stay productive during work,

1. Find comfortable environment.
2. Eliminate distractions.
3. Set a clear goal.
4. Schedule breaks every 45 minutes if possible.
5. Work.

Also, ask for help as soon as you need it.

= What is important for you as a future technical writer:  

* Doing your research, you have to know what you are writing about (read up on the subject matter). 

* As a TW you are not working alone! Talking to your colleagues is mandatory, because they give you feedback that is needed. 

* Create user personas – fictional characters that represent a user (Do not forget that you are going to be a content creator)
