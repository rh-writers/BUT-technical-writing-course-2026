== 3. Writing skills:

Technical writer should aim for straight forward documentation, without excess fluff, but keeping the text accurate, clear, and effective (ACE)

*3.1 Utilization:*

- clear language
- consistent terminology
- short sentences
- logical structure

*3.2 Forms of tech writing:*

  . Manuals, guides
  . Solutions or troubleshooting
  . Patch notes, release notes

*3.3 Formatting:*

Follow a specific style guide so your documentation is:

- reliable
- consistent
- scannable
- findable

*3.4 Style guides:*

Examples:

. https://support.apple.com/guide/applestyleguide/welcome/web[Apple style guide]
. https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/resources[Google style guide]
. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/[Microsoft style guide]
