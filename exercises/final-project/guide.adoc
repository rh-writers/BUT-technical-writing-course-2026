== Communication and Research

The goal of communication and research is to gather accurate information and share it clearly with the intended audience. Good communication helps users understand documentation, while research ensures the information is reliable and useful :

basic writing skills

critical thinking

attention to detail

understanding of the target audience

Procedure:

identify the purpose of the documentation

research reliable sources

verify information from multiple sources

organize findings

communicate information clearly and concisely

review and update content regularly

Research:

Use trusted sources such as:

official documentation

technical articles

academic publications

industry standards

Communication:

Focus on the reader's needs.

Use:

clear language

consistent terminology

short sentences

logical structure

Best practices:

write for the user

avoid unnecessary information

verify facts before publishing

maintain consistency

keep documentation up to date
