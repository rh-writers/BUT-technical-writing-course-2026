= How to become a technical writer step-by-step
== Avoid these common mistakes:
* **Overuse of passive voice:** Mainly aim for the active voice to make your documentation more direct and clear.
* **Self-referential text:** Avoid meta-phrases like "This document describes..." or "In this section, I will show..."
* **Anthropomorphism:** Do not attribute human emotions or behaviors to software (avoid phrases like "the program became confused").
* **Inconsistent formatting:** Watch out for formatting consistency throughout the document.
* **Tutorial vs. Technical writing:** Differentiate between an educational tutorial and professional tech writing.
* **Colorful analogies:** Avoid using colorful analogies.
* **Informal language:** Avoid informal slang and curse words.
* **Pronouns & Tone:** Avoid first-person plural or conversational phrasing like "Let's use...". Use direct, imperative instructions in the 2nd person.
* **Stating the objective:** Reverse the sentence structure. For example, "Use command XY to [achieve goal]" is better structured as "To [achieve goal], use command XY."

== Follow these human attributes (Soft skills):
* Be open-minded
* Be accurate
* Write concisely
* Write in a beginner-friendly style
* Be flexible and adaptable
* Have a strong team spirit
* Be communicative
* Seek guidance from others

== Objectives of technical writing style:
* **Accessibility:** Comprehensible for all users.
* **Readability:** Structured to allow users to understand the text quickly.
* **Minimalism:** Reduction of fluff.
* **Modularity:** Broken down into small blocks (multiple parts).
* **Reusability:** Reuse of steps for future purposes.
* **Research:** Do research before writing technical documentation.
* **Consistency:** Stick to one writing style (link:https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/welcome/[Microsoft Writing Style Guide]).
